## Interpretation
The two idempotency keys are consecutive minute slots (29869586 at 02:26, 29869587 at
02:27), i.e. the same failing mailbox was re-enqueued on two back-to-back minutes and
each job burned its full 5-attempt budget. This is the real-world shape of the D1 defect
(no backoff for a permanently failing mailbox) captured before the fix was deployed at
03:15. After the fixture set all mailboxes autoSync=false, the scheduler stopped
producing scheduled syncs; no further jobs were created.
Errors show the pre-fix placeholder reason ({"code": "JOB_FAILED", "message": "Job
execution failed"}); the fix records a bounded, redacted reason plus job/kind/mailbox/attempt.
